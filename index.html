About me

I am a PhD researcher in astrophysics at IIT Indore, investigating the distribution of ionised baryonic matter in the Universe using fast radio bursts (FRBs).

My research focuses on using FRB dispersion measures to probe the circumgalactic medium (CGM) and intergalactic medium (IGM), addressing the missing-baryon problem. I work with cosmological simulations, halo gas-density models, and forecasts for future FRB surveys to investigate how observations can constrain the baryon content of dark matter haloes and the physical properties of circumgalactic gas.
