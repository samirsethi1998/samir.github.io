About me

I am a PhD researcher in Astrophysics & Cosmology at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, investigating the distribution of ionised baryonic matter in the Universe using fast radio bursts (FRBs).

My research uses FRB dispersion measures to probe the circumgalactic medium (CGM) and intergalactic medium (IGM), with the aim of improving our understanding of the missing-baryon problem. I work with cosmological simulations, halo gas-density models, FRB population modelling, and forecasts for future surveys, including the Square Kilometre Array (SKA).

PhD Researcher · IIT Indore, India · Supervisor: Dr. Priyanka Singh
