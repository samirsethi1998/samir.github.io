About

I study how ordinary matter is distributed in the Universe, using fast radio bursts as probes of the ionised gas they pass through.

I am a PhD student at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, supervised by Dr. Priyanka Singh. I welcome discussions and collaborations on fast radio bursts, the CGM–IGM, and cosmological baryon studies.

Research

Fast radio bursts

Using dispersion measures to trace ionised matter along cosmological sightlines, with FRB population modelling.

Missing baryons

Mapping where ordinary matter sits between galaxies and across the cosmic web.

Circumgalactic medium

Modelling ionised gas around dark matter haloes and its share of the observed dispersion measure.

Simulations and forecasts

Combining cosmological simulations and halo gas-density models to forecast constraints from future surveys, including the Square Kilometre Array (SKA).
