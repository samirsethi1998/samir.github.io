About

I study how ordinary matter is distributed in the Universe, using fast radio bursts as probes of the ionised gas they travel through.

I am a PhD researcher in Astrophysics & Cosmology at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, supervised by Dr. Priyanka Singh. My work uses FRB dispersion measures to probe the circumgalactic medium (CGM) and the intergalactic medium (IGM), with the aim of improving our understanding of the missing-baryon problem.

I work with cosmological simulations, halo gas-density models, FRB population modelling, and forecasts for future surveys, including the Square Kilometre Array (SKA).
